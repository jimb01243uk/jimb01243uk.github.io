Home Assistant Nest Bridge

A personal, non-commercial integration between Google Nest devices and a self-hosted Home Assistant instance.

This application is used by a single individual to connect their own Google Nest cameras and doorbell to their own Home Assistant server, running on hardware in their home. It is not a product, it is not distributed, and it is not offered to other users.

What it does

Using Google's Smart Device Management API, the application reads device status and receives camera events so that Home Assistant can:

  • display camera and doorbell status on a private dashboard
  • trigger household automations, such as a notification when a person is detected late at night
  • show a live camera stream on request

Privacy

No data is collected, sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. Everything stays on the owner's own hardware. See the privacy policy for detail.
